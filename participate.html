HCRF is a Section 8 public-interest institution and India’s first Craft Policy Think Tank dedicated to Kashmir. We work at the intersection of research, policy, and practice to protect traditional handicrafts, strengthen artisan livelihoods, and advance sustainable craft ecosystems rooted in heritage. Through evidence-based research, policy advocacy, institutional partnerships, artisan support, documentation, and public engagement, HCRF works to preserve Kashmir’s craft knowledge.
