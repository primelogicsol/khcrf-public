Campaigning for Artisan Empowerment and Craft Protection

HCRF’s policy campaigns are designed to mobilize informed support for issues affecting the Kashmiri handicraft sector. These campaigns connect research, advocacy, and public participation to influence meaningful policy outcomes.

By engaging policymakers, institutions, artisans, and supporters, we work to ensure that craft traditions are protected not only as heritage, but as viable and dignified livelihoods.

Current campaign priorities include:

  • Combating counterfeiting and protecting authenticity

  • Gender-inclusive craft policies and women artisan empowerment

  • Environmental sustainability in handicraft production

  • Artisan health, safety, and fair working standards

  • Economic resilience through fair trade and direct-to-artisan models

  • Intergenerational skill transfer and youth engagement

Campaigning is not about slogans. It is about sustained effort, credible research, and collective action that leads to better policy and stronger protections for artisans.
