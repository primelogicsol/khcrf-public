RESEARCH & POLICY

Kashmir Craft Policy Advocacy

A strategic effort to protect artisans, preserve heritage, and shape fair policy.

Kashmir’s craft traditions are not just cultural symbols. They are livelihoods, ecosystems of skill, and inherited knowledge passed through generations.

Today, these crafts face growing threats from counterfeit products, weak enforcement, market exclusion, and policy gaps that leave artisans unprotected.

Through the Kashmir Craft Policy Advocacy initiative, we work with researchers, artisans, institutions, and supporters to influence policies that safeguard authenticity, ensure fair access to markets, and support sustainable craft economies.

Our focus includes:

  • Counterfeit prevention and GI protection

  • Artisan rights and fair compensation

  • Sustainable and ethical production

  • Market access and digital inclusion

  • Gender equity and skill preservation

Your participation strengthens this effort. Whether through advocacy, collaboration, or support, your voice helps shape policies that will define the future of Kashmir’s craft sector.
