# State of Kashmir Crafts Assessment Platform: Vision Document

## The "Why"
The State of Kashmir Crafts (SKC) Assessment Platform is not merely a website or a data-collection tool. It is an **Institutional Information System**. 

It exists to capture, govern, verify, and preserve the lived experiences, economic realities, and cultural heritage of Kashmir's artisan community. By transforming localized, often fragmented artisanal knowledge into a rigorously audited, publicly transparent intelligence layer, the platform empowers policymakers, researchers, and global stakeholders to make evidence-based decisions that protect and elevate the crafts ecosystem.

## 5–10 Year Horizon
Over the next decade, this platform is intended to become the canonical, longitudinal record of the Kashmir crafts sector. It will evolve from a cyclical assessment tool into a continuous **Knowledge Graph**—a living institutional asset linking stakeholders, crafts, districts, policies, environmental indicators, and historical assessments. 

It will serve as the trusted semantic foundation upon which AI research assistants, predictive analytics, and automated policy briefings are built.

## Primary Users
1. **Artisans & Guilds:** To submit evidence, voice challenges, and track policy outcomes.
2. **HCRF Administrators:** To govern data, manage workflows, and synthesize institutional intelligence.
3. **Researchers & Academics:** To query certified, anonymized data for longitudinal studies.
4. **Government & Policymakers:** To consume verified recommendations and demographic trends for resource allocation.
5. **The Public:** To access transparent, aggregate progress metrics without compromising individual privacy.

## Core Optimization Directives
Future contributors must optimize for the following:
- **Trust over Speed:** A slow, verified workflow is always superior to a fast, unverified data pipeline.
- **Traceability over Convenience:** Every state change, public metric, or AI-generated insight must trace back to a certified, audited origin.
- **Institutional Memory:** Code and data models should be designed to survive turnover in personnel.

## Uncompromising Principles
1. **The Single Source of Truth:** One workflow engine, one status enum, one normalization layer, one public aggregation engine. Duplicate metric calculation is strictly forbidden.
2. **Absolute Privacy:** Raw data, participant identities, and internal notes shall never bypass the Public Intelligence Layer.
3. **Certified Data Foundation:** Every advanced capability (AI, Predictive Analytics, Report Generation) must consume strictly from the certified RC-1 data layer. They may never invent their own interpretation of the underlying data.
