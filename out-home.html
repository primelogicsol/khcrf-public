WelcomeKashmir Hamadan Craft Revival Foundation

An ecosystem where art and artisans flourish

We are undertaking a pioneering initiative to develop a comprehensive policy framework that enables artisans, safeguards traditional techniques, and forges a path forward in the global market through rigorous scientific inquiry.

"The Foundation works as a think tank, engaging in research, policy analysis, and advocacy to protect the rich heritage of Kashmiri crafts while addressing the modern challenges faced by artisans."

OUR MISSION & APPROACH

Reviving Heritage. Empowering Artisans. Securing The Future Of Kashmir Crafts.

The Kashmir Hamadan Craft Revival Foundation (KHCRF) works at the intersection of research, preservation, policy, enterprise development, and public engagement to strengthen Kashmir's living craft traditions.

We believe that preserving a craft requires more than protecting products. It requires protecting knowledge, supporting artisans, strengthening institutions, enabling markets, and building long-term systems that allow traditional craftsmanship to thrive in a changing world.

Research & Intelligence

Industry research, market intelligence, policy analysis, and evidence-based insights supporting the future of Kashmir's handicraft sector.

Documentation & Preservation

Recording traditional knowledge, artisan histories, cultural practices, techniques, and endangered craft traditions for future generations.

Policy & Advocacy

Advancing legislative awareness, policy reform, artisan protection, and institutional support through research-driven advocacy.

Artisan Development

Supporting artisans through education, capacity building, recognition programs, and sustainable livelihood initiatives.

Business & Enterprise Support

Helping enterprises improve quality, certification, market readiness, credibility, and long-term business sustainability.

Public Engagement & Partnerships

Connecting communities, institutions, researchers, businesses, and supporters through collaborative programs and campaigns.

"For more than seven centuries, Kashmir's crafts have carried stories of skill, resilience, creativity, and cultural identity. KHCRF exists to ensure these traditions continue to inspire, support livelihoods, and remain relevant for generations to come."

SURVEY & FIELD ANALYSIS

State of Kashmir Crafts
Assessment 2026

We are conducting the most comprehensive field-level study of the Kashmiri craft sector. Measuring production indices, artisan wages, cooperative structures, and authentic GI pashmina exports to construct a resilient policy roadmap.

"

This is the first time in the history of Kashmiri handicrafts that a field-level census of this scale has been launched. It builds a definitive data framework to secure fair wages, block counterfeit trade, and establish clear provenance tracking directly benefiting the weavers.

KHCRF INDEPENDENT CRAFT VERIFICATION

Build Verified Trust for Your Craft Practice, Business or Institution

Submit your claims and supporting evidence for independent KHCRF verification against applicable craft, provenance, responsible-practice and compliance standards.

KHCRF’s verification framework supports artisans, businesses and institutions across the Kashmir craft sector. Submitted claims are reviewed against applicable factors including authenticity and provenance, responsible practices, worker safeguards, traceability, sustainability and operational presence. Supporting evidence is independently reviewed, with ground verification conducted where required.

AFTER KHCRF VERIFICATION

Once KHCRF completes independent verification, the verified findings are securely shared with Craftlore. Craftlore uses those findings to calculate your verified Performance Trust Score, determine your rank within the relevant entity category, and strengthen your discoverability across its trade-intelligence ecosystem.

Verified PTS

Evidence-backed performance score calculated by Craftlore.

Category Ranking

Ranked separately among Artisans, Businesses, or Institutions.

Greater Discoverability

Helps buyers, partners and institutions identify verified entities with stronger trust signals.

CRAFTLORE RECOGNITION TIERS

Gold, Silver and Bronze are Craftlore administrative recognition tiers for qualifying verified entities. They are separate from KHCRF verification and are not automatically awarded by score alone.

How recognition works
KHCRF verifies evidence → Craftlore evaluates trust and ranking → Craftlore may assign recognition tiers

Meaning

Eligibility

Visibility Benefit

Global Impact
Building a Sustainable Future

KHCRF works alongside nonprofit, knowledge, cultural, technology and independent commercial platforms that address different parts of the craft ecosystem. These organizations retain their own governance, operational and commercial responsibilities. KHCRF itself does not act as a marketplace, reseller, broker or transaction intermediary. Participation in an external commercial platform is independent of KHCRF evaluation, research, recognition, membership and institutional services.

KHCRF is a Section 8 public-interest institution and India’s first Craft Policy Think Tank dedicated to Kashmir. We work at the intersection of research, policy, and practice to protect traditional handicrafts, strengthen artisan livelihoods, and advance sustainable craft ecosystems rooted in heritage. Through evidence-based research, policy advocacy, institutional partnerships, artisan support, documentation, and public engagement, KHCRF works to preserve Kashmir’s craft knowledge.
